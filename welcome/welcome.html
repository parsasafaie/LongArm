Welcome to LongArm

Connects ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek with your local tools (MCP). Just follow these steps; the guide updates itself as you make progress.

  1. 1

    Install the launcher

    A browser cannot start programs on its own. The launcher is a small process that is installed only once, starts with your session and lets you turn the bridge on, off and restart it from this extension.

    1. Open the extension folder and go into server.
    2. Double-click install.cmd and wait until it says «Done».
    1. Open a terminal inside the extension folder.
    2. Run bash server/install.sh once — it installs what is missing and registers the automatic start.

    Prefer the terminal? python server/install_service.py

  2. 2

    Turn on the bridge

    The bridge is the program that publishes your MCP servers. You can control it from here or from the extension menu in the toolbar.

  3. 3

    Your MCP servers

    They come from server/servers.json — the terminal server ships registered. Add an entry (name, server_path, guide_path, port) and save; it appears here within seconds. Turn on the ones you want and choose their permission. There is no hard limit, but keeping few active costs fewer tokens (one is recommended).

    • 4

      Try it in your favourite chat

      Copy the rules below and paste them into a Project (ChatGPT, Claude) or a Skill (Gemini) — in a Project, start the chat inside it; in Gemini, pick the skill with / in any chat. Then open your chat with a button. They are generated from the extension, so this text is always the one it expects. DeepSeek has no place for them: see instructions/README.md.
